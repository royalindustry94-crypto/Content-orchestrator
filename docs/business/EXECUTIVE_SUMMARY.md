# Executive Summary — Content Orchestrator

**Document type:** Business foundation roll-up (≤2 pages)  
**Audience:** Founder / advisors  
**Date context:** 2026 — engineering foundation finalizing; this pack defines commercial direction only.

---

## What we are

Content Orchestrator is a **vertical content production control plane**: plan → generate → **mandatory Human Review Gate** → export, with **hard spend controls** and **multi-tenant workspace isolation**.

We are **not** a horizontal iPaaS (Zapier/Make/n8n), **not** an autonomous AI employee (Lindy/Relevance-class), and **not** “AI that posts for you.” We sell **governed throughput** for teams that already feel pain from wrong publishes and runaway AI/video cost.

---

## Why now

AI content tooling is abundant; **accountability is scarce**. Agencies and in-house content ops stitch fragile automation graphs, then absorb brand and budget damage. The market is cooling on autonomy theater and warming to **HITL + cost control** — Relay-like ideas validate HITL broadly; we specialize them for content production.

---

## Who buys

**Primary ICP:** Content agencies and serious in-house content ops (multi-brand or multi-client).  
**Buyer:** Ops lead / founder / content manager who owns tools and budget.  
**Anti-ICP:** Solo creators seeking one-click autonomous posting; teams that refuse review.

**Budget (assumption):** Starter ~$49/mo, Pro ~$199/mo, Agency ~$499/mo, Enterprise custom — priced above generic task automation because governance and isolation are the product.

---

## How we make money

Subscription software with **BYOK-first** generation (protects margin). Optional platform-metered usage only with strict markup and caps. **Gate and spend controls are not disableable** — they are the SKU.

**Margin thesis (assumption):** Software gross margin ≥80% on BYOK-heavy accounts; never subsidize unlimited generation to fake growth.

---

## How we go to market

Bootstrapped, ROI-ranked: **SEO + YouTube → partnerships/outbound → referral/affiliate →** community. **Defer paid ads** until activation and CAC are known. Message: control and safety, not magic.

---

## Competitive position

| We win on | We lose on |
|-----------|------------|
| Mandatory review, spend ledger, agency workspaces | Connector count, full autonomy, self-host OSS, lowest price |

**Moat (realistic):** Operational embedding of Gate + spend + tenancy — not UI polish or slogans.

---

## 12-month intent

Q1 trustworthy paid core loop → Q2 activation/templates/multi-workspace → Q3 selective integrations + expansion revenue → Q4 retention/compliance door for Enterprise. **Invariants never slip for features.**

---

## Risks that can kill us

1. Cross-tenant breach  
2. Spend-control failure  
3. Gate bypass  
4. Negative AI margins / runway burn  
5. Chasing anti-ICP with autonomy features  

Mitigations are documented in `RISK_REGISTER.md` and must stay engineering non-negotiables.

---

## Commercial viability — verdict

**Viable as a bootstrapped niche B2B SaaS if** we stay vertical, enforce Gate/spend/tenancy, price for agencies, keep infra and COGS lean, and acquire via content + outbound rather than paid hype.

**Not viable if** we pivot to horizontal automation, promise full autonomy, or grow on subsidized generation.

**Expected shape:** Slow, opinionated, high-trust sales; smaller TAM than Zapier; better ownership and margins than agent hype. Profitability over unicorn narrative.
