# Go-to-Market Strategy — Content Orchestrator

**Audience:** Founder + growth  
**Stance:** Bootstrapped. Prioritize ROI over vanity. Prefer channels that attract buyers who care about **control**, not novelty.

---

| 2 | YouTube | High | Medium time | 2–6 months | Trust + demos of Gate |

## 2. Channel playbooks

### 2.1 SEO (priority 1)

**Goal:** Own “content automation with human review / spend control / agency video ops” queries.

**Content pillars:**
1. Problem: runaway AI video spend, wrong publishes, multi-client chaos
2. Comparison: Make/Zapier/n8n vs content-native control plane
3. How-to: review workflows, brand kits, spend caps, workspace isolation
4. Case-style: “agency ops” process docs (even without logos initially — process templates)

**Tactics:**
- Long-form guides + comparison pages (honest, not keyword spam)
- Glossary + playbooks under `/docs` public marketing later
- Capture search demand for competitor + “review gate” adjacent terms
- Technical SEO only after a real marketing site exists

**Assumption:** Founder writes or heavily edits; AI-only SEO underperforms for B2B trust.

### 2.2 YouTube (priority 2)

**Formats that convert:**
- Screen demos: Review Gate in action; spend blocked; workspace isolation
- “Build an agency video pipeline without Zap spaghetti”
- Honest teardown of competitor workflows (fair, factual)

**Cadence (bootstrapped):** 1 quality video / week max; consistency > volume.

**CTA:** Trial with spend caps on; Gate cannot be skipped.

### 2.3 TikTok (priority 10)

**Role:** Awareness only. Short clips from YouTube. Do not expect enterprise or agency closes from TikTok alone.

**Risk:** Audience mismatch (creators want magic buttons; we sell control).

### 2.4 Reddit (priority 3)

**Where:** r/SaaS, r/Agency, r/marketing, r/Entrepreneur, niche automation subs — **with value first**.

**Rules:**
- Answer ops questions; link sparingly
- Never launch-spam
- Use AMA / case discussion only when product can be shown

**Risk:** Account bans destroy channel equity.

### 2.5 X (priority 9)

**Use:** Founder narrative, shipping logs, Gate philosophy, thread demos.

**Avoid:** Engagement bait, crypto-style AI hype.

### 2.6 Discord (priority 8)

**Use:** Customer community after paying users exist; or join agency/ops Discords as helpful peer.

**Avoid:** Building a Discord as GTM day-one (maintenance tax).

### 2.7 Influencers (priority 11)

**Only when:**
- Creator has **agency / ops / AI tooling** audience (not lifestyle)
- Deal is performance-based or product-for-content with clear brief
- Demo script centers Gate + spend — not “AI makes videos for you”

**Cap:** Small experiments; kill if CAC > 1/3 of expected LTV.

### 2.8 Affiliate program (priority 6)

**Timing:** After product reliability and clear positioning.

**Design (assumption):**
- 20% of first 12 months subscription (software only), or fixed bounty
- No payout on usage markup if BYOK-heavy
- Require disclosure; ban dark-pattern landing pages

**Quality bar:** Affiliates who sell “unlimited AI video” are terminated.

### 2.9 Referral system (priority 5)

**Design:**
- Credit toward subscription (not cash initially) — e.g. 1 month Starter credit for referrer + referee after first paid invoice
- Agency: higher credit or seat unlock

**Prerequisite:** NPS / satisfaction signal; do not launch referral on broken onboarding.

### 2.10 Cold outreach (priority 7)

**Targets:** Agency ops leads, content managers at DTC brands, boutique AI agencies.

**Message angle:** Cost control + publish safety + multi-client isolation — not “AI content factory.”

**Volume:** Founder-led, highly personalized; 20–40 quality touches/week beats 500 spam.

**Evidence:** Loom of Gate + spend block > PDF pitch.

### 2.11 Paid ads (priority 12 — defer)

**When:** After knowing activation rate, demo → paid conversion, and organic CAC.

**Then test:** Google Search on high-intent terms; LinkedIn sparingly for Agency.

**Never early:** Broad Meta/TikTok prospecting for this ICP.

### 2.12 Partnerships (priority 4)

**Partners:**
- Video editors / fractional CMOs
- AI agency consultants who hate owning infra
- Complementary tools (scheduling, analytics) — not direct competitors
- Freelancer communities that serve brands

**Offer:** Co-marketing, referral fee, white-label path later (Enterprise only).

## 3. Messaging by stage

| Stage | Message |
|-------|---------|
| Awareness | “AI video spend and wrong publishes are ops problems.” |
| Consideration | “Orchestrate production with a mandatory Review Gate and hard spend caps.” |
| Decision | “Multi-tenant isolation, auditability, BYOK — built for agencies.” |
| Expansion | “Add workspaces, brands, and operators without losing control.” |

---

## 4. Launch sequence (bootstrapped)

1. **Private alpha** — 5–15 design partners (agencies preferred); free/discount for feedback + case permission.
2. **Public beta** — Starter/Pro live; Gate + spend enforced; marketing site + demo video.
3. **Content engine** — SEO + YouTube weekly; Reddit helpful presence.
4. **Outbound** — Targeted agency list.
5. **Referral** — After 20+ happy paying accounts.
6. **Affiliate** — After referral works.
7. **Paid** — Only with unit economics.

---

## 5. Budget allocation (illustrative Year 1, cash-constrained)

**Assumption:** Founder time is the main budget.

| Bucket | % of growth time/cash |
|--------|------------------------|
| Product reliability + onboarding | 40% |
| SEO + YouTube | 30% |
| Outreach + partnerships | 20% |
| Experiments (affiliates, small ads) | 10% |

Cash ads: **$0** until funnel metrics exist; then small capped tests.

---

## 6. Anti-patterns

- Launching on Product Hunt as primary strategy without retention
- “AI agent will replace your content team” messaging
- Buying fake social proof
- Scaling support before self-serve docs for Gate/spend
- Channel hopping weekly

---

## 7. Success criteria (first 6–12 months)

- Organic + outbound produce majority of trials
- Activation (first reviewed export) measurable and improving
- CAC payback < 6 months on Pro/Agency (assumption target)
- At least 2–3 reference customers willing to speak

---

*Assumptions are labeled. Channel ROI is directional, not a forecast.*
