# Lumora Founding-Tester Recruitment Plan

**Target:** build a qualified pool of **10 founding testers** for a controlled, non-paying private beta. The purpose is to learn whether the one Lumora workflow is repeatable and valuable, not to manufacture customer validation, testimonials, or a public-launch narrative.

> No outreach has been sent under this plan. Channel access, posting rights, marketplace eligibility, and community rules must be checked by the human operator before any outreach.

## 1. Ideal tester profile

The best tester is an operator who already turns ideas into repeatable content and can compare Lumora against a real existing workflow. Audience size is secondary to repeatability, rights awareness, willingness to review output, and ability to give candid feedback.

| Priority profile | Required signs of fit | Why it is useful |
|---|---|---|
| Faceless YouTube operator | Publishes recurring long-form or Shorts content; can describe idea-to-script/review process; has a clear reviewer. | Tests structured, repeatable content production with a meaningful approval step. |
| TikTok or Instagram creator | Produces recurring short-form content and can name a target platform/content goal. | Tests speed, platform-fit feedback, and publish-ready handoff. |
| Small AI-content agency | Has 1–5 operators, recurring client or internal workflows, and authority to nominate one reviewer. | Tests orchestration and review boundaries without expanding into a marketplace. |
| Multi-tool AI creator | Already combines more than one content, voice, image, video, editing, or analytics tool and can articulate friction. | Provides an informed comparison with a current multi-tool workflow. |

The beta requires an adult participant who can control their own content decisions, has a use case within the permitted workflow, and accepts that Lumora produces **publish-ready output only**. The human tester retains responsibility for any external platform publication and rights decision.

## 2. Disqualification criteria

Do not recruit a candidate if any condition below is present. A polite decline is better than accepting a tester whose expected workflow requires bypassing safety controls.

| Disqualify when the candidate… | Reason |
|---|---|
| Wants autonomous posting, growth guarantees, follower acquisition, or paid-ad optimisation. | Those outcomes are outside the beta workflow and increase safety/claims risk. |
| Cannot identify a reviewer for outputs. | Human Review Gate is mandatory. |
| Plans to upload material they do not control or cannot assess for rights. | The beta cannot treat generated output as rights clearance. |
| Wants access for prohibited, deceptive, unsafe, political persuasion, adult, or otherwise policy-incompatible use. | The beta must stay within the documented platform and safety boundary. |
| Cannot provide a real baseline workflow or commit to one feedback session. | No valid comparison or learning loop exists. |
| Treats participation as a request for free agency services, custom development, or immediate integration. | Scope must remain one workflow. |
| Requests live billing, automated external publishing, or broad team access. | These controls are intentionally disabled or out of scope. |

## 3. Outreach channels and operating rules

Use only channels where the operator is permitted to contact candidates. Prefer warm introductions and communities where a genuine contribution is possible; never scrape, mass-DM, or impersonate creators. The official resources below confirm that creator communities or marketplaces exist; they do **not** grant Lumora access or permission to solicit members.

| Channel | Practical use | Gate before outreach |
|---|---|---|
| Warm introductions from creators, agencies, editors, and tool users | Highest-trust starting point; ask for an opt-in introduction rather than forwarding contact details. | Referrer confirms the candidate is suitable and willing to be contacted. |
| YouTube Creator Community / Creator Collective / Help Community | Discover creator workflows and participate in permitted conversations; invite only where rules allow. YouTube identifies official creator-community, event, forum, and research-study resources. [1] | Read community rules; obtain moderator approval where necessary; never post a disguised advertisement. |
| TikTok One / Creator Marketplace | Source qualified short-form creators or production partners where the Founder has eligible access. TikTok describes collaboration and creator-discovery tools, with eligibility requirements that vary by account and region. [2] | Confirm account eligibility, region, permissions, and applicable commercial terms. |
| Meta Creator Marketplace | Source Instagram creator prospects where the Founder has eligible business access. Meta describes discovery, evaluation, and direct contact functionality for eligible participants. [3] | Confirm business eligibility and permissions; do not imply Meta endorsement. |
| Personal creator/agency networks and relevant professional communities | Use a transparent invitation from a known operator. | Respect each group's rules and avoid bulk posting. |

## 4. Outreach sequence and templates

Personalise every message with one observed workflow fact. Do not claim that Lumora saves time, increases growth, is compliant, or is ready for public launch; those claims do not yet have beta evidence.

### Template A — warm introduction request

> Hi **[referrer]** — I am recruiting a very small private-beta group for Lumora, a tool testing one workflow: idea/input → generation → Human Review Gate → publish-ready output. We are looking for creators or small teams who already have a recurring workflow and will give candid feedback after a real attempt. Would **[candidate]** be open to an opt-in introduction? Please do not share their details unless they say yes. We are not offering auto-posting, growth guarantees, or paid billing in this beta.

### Template B — direct creator invitation

> Hi **[name]** — I noticed you regularly create **[specific content type]**. We are inviting 10 operators to a narrow, non-paying Lumora private beta. The test is one workflow: bring one idea, generate/process it, make a named Human Review Gate decision, and leave with a publish-ready output. It is **not** automatic posting, a growth service, or a request to hand over platform credentials.
>
> We would ask for one real workflow attempt, a short follow-up, and candid feedback even if the output is rejected. If this is relevant, may I send a 5-minute fit screen? If not, no action needed.

### Template C — small agency invitation

> Hi **[name]** — we are testing Lumora with a small number of content operators who already coordinate idea-to-review work. The beta is deliberately narrow: one content item at a time, named reviewer, no automatic external publishing, and no live billing. We are looking for an agency that can nominate one operator and one reviewer, run a real internal workflow, and discuss quality, review friction, time, and value. Would a 15-minute fit call be useful?

### Template D — follow-up (one time only)

> Hi **[name]** — following up once in case this was relevant. The invitation is for a small, non-paying beta focused on one reviewable content workflow, not a sales commitment. If it is not a fit, I will close the loop. If it is, reply with your content type and whether you can nominate a reviewer.

If there is no response after one follow-up, mark the lead closed; do not continue messaging.

## 5. Tester scorecard

Score candidates before inviting. A score does not replace judgment: any disqualification condition overrides the total.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Recurring workflow | No current content workflow. | Occasional/manual workflow. | Repeats a defined workflow weekly or more. | 3 |
| Persona fit | Outside target profiles. | Partial fit. | Direct YouTube/TikTok/Instagram/agency/multi-tool fit. | 3 |
| Reviewer availability | None. | Unclear. | Named reviewer can evaluate exact outputs. | 3 |
| Feedback quality | Wants access only. | Can give general feedback. | Can compare against baseline and commit to structured feedback. | 3 |
| Rights/safety awareness | Cannot explain input rights. | Partial understanding. | Clear boundary and willingness to reject uncertain input. | 2 |
| Scope alignment | Wants out-of-scope functionality. | Some scope questions. | Accepts publish-ready-only, no live billing, no auto-posting. | 3 |
| Time availability | Cannot attempt workflow within 14 days. | Uncertain. | Can start within 7 days and follow up. | 2 |

**Weighted score:** sum of `(rating × weight)`. Invite candidates at **26 or higher** if no disqualifier applies. Hold candidates scoring 20–25 only if the first cohort needs a specific profile. Decline below 20.

## 6. Fit interview questions

1. Walk me through the last content item you produced from idea to final review. Which steps were manual or used different tools?
2. What content type and platform would you use for one beta attempt?
3. Who would review the exact final output, and what would cause them to reject it?
4. What inputs do you plan to provide, and how do you know you can use them?
5. What currently takes the most active time or causes the most rework?
6. What would make you stop using the workflow after one attempt?
7. Can you complete one real workflow and a 15-minute feedback conversation within 7 days of access?
8. How would you recognise that an output is publish-ready but not yet published?
9. What other AI/content tools are involved today, and where do handoffs fail?
10. After you have used the workflow, what pricing model would you expect if it reliably solved the stated problem? This is exploratory, not a price commitment.

## 7. Onboarding and feedback cadence

| Timing | Action | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before invitation | Score candidate and record disqualification check. | Recruiter/operator | Lead record. |
| Invitation accepted | Send `BETA_TESTER_ONBOARDING.md`, confirm reviewer and scope. | Operator | Acceptance time and workspace ID. |
| Day 0 | Conduct short setup; capture baseline time and intended workflow. | Operator + tester | Operator log. |
| Day 1–7 | Support one intentional workflow; do not expand scope to “make it work.” | Operator | Run/gate IDs and outcome. |
| Within 24 hours of HRG outcome | Send feedback form and schedule follow-up. | Operator | Completed form or non-response. |
| Day 7–14 | Check repeat use and conduct WTP conversation only if a real workflow was completed. | Founder/product owner | Interview note, not a fabricated conversion claim. |
| Weekly | Review funnel, failure reasons, quality/rejection reasons, time, cost, repeat use, and WTP evidence. | Founder + engineering | Cohort review. |

## 8. Success and failure thresholds

The plan starts with a 10-person qualified prospect pool, but a safe beta should admit a small first wave and only expand when operations are stable. Metrics are not valid until the denominators in the validation plan exist.

| Signal | Continue / expand carefully | Pause / investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Tester quality | At least 10 scored prospects; each admitted tester meets score and disqualification rules. | Candidates seek auto-posting, lack reviewer/rights fit, or cannot attempt the workflow. |
| Activation | Establish baseline across first 10 accepted invitations. | Material onboarding, authorisation, or HRG-reach failure. |
| Workflow completion | At least 70% after 30 intentional starts. | Below 50% for two weekly cohorts. |
| Accepted output | At least 50% after 30 review decisions, segmented by content type. | Below 30% or repeated rejection cause not triaged. |
| Repeat use | At least 40% of activated workspaces after 10 activated workspaces. | Below 20% after 10, absent a clear correctable cause. |
| Technical failure | Below 5% after 30 starts and no Critical/High safety failure. | 10% or more, any tenant/HRG/spend/uncontrolled-publish defect, or unexplained duplicate provider effect. |
| Value / WTP | Real use followed by a documented price-sensitive commitment, paid conversion, or signed intent. | Generic praise or hypothetical survey answer only; do not call it validation. |

## 9. Lead data discipline

Use `docs/templates/beta_tester_leads_template.csv`. Record only business-contact data a candidate has provided or consented to share. Do not store credentials, private tokens, payment data, unnecessary personal details, or sensitive source material. Mark contact source and consent; respect opt-outs immediately.
