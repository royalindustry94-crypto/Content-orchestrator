# Lumora Private-Beta Tester Onboarding

Welcome to Lumora's controlled private beta. The beta evaluates one workflow only:

> **Idea/input → generation → processing → Human Review Gate → approval or rejection → publish-ready output → analytics review.**

Lumora is not a marketplace, CRM, audience-growth service, or automated publishing tool in this beta. Your feedback is valuable whether your output is approved, rejected, revised, or fails technically; please report the actual outcome rather than trying to make the workflow appear successful.

## 1. Before access is enabled

Your operator will confirm a workspace, one named reviewer, the intended beta use case, and a contact method for support. You must not share access, credentials, or outputs containing personal, confidential, copyrighted, or third-party material unless you have the necessary rights and permission to process it.

| You will provide | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| A short description of your recurring content workflow | To determine whether your use case fits the beta wedge. |
| One intended platform | To frame the output and disclosure review. It does not authorize automatic posting. |
| A reviewer name | Every output needs a named human decision. |
| A rights statement for supplied material | The reviewer needs to make an informed approval decision. |
| A baseline time estimate | This is used only to compare your own before/after workflow, not to claim universal time savings. |

## 2. What a successful beta session looks like

A successful session is **not** merely a generated draft. It is an intentional workflow that reaches a recorded Human Review Gate decision for the exact content version. A rejected output is still a successful learning event if you give a reason. A publish-ready output means the internal review and eligibility controls are complete; it does not mean Lumora has posted anything externally.

| Step | Your action | Expected system behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Idea/input | Provide one clear idea, audience, intended platform, and content goal. | Creates or supports one intentional workflow. |
| Generation/processing | Review interim content as requested; do not start unrelated parallel experiments in the same beta session. | Shows current stage and any technical failure. |
| Human Review Gate | Review the exact output and choose approve or reject. Add a reason whenever possible. | Records a version-specific, non-transferable decision. |
| Publish-ready handoff | If approved, download/copy the exact approved output and complete your own platform publication process. | Does not publish automatically. |
| Analytics/feedback | Share the outcome you consent to share and complete the short feedback form. | Associates feedback with the beta session rather than inventing performance data. |

## 3. Your review responsibilities

Before you approve, confirm that the exact version is suitable for your intended use, that supplied inputs can be used, that any required synthetic-media disclosure is understood, and that it is not a near-duplicate you do not want to publish. If you are unsure, reject or pause the item and explain why. You remain responsible for your own final publication and platform-account decisions.

Do not approve one item in order to reuse that approval elsewhere. Each Human Review Gate is bound to one content item and workflow run. Do not ask the operator to bypass a rejection, a timed-out gate, a budget limit, or a platform-policy control.

## 4. What to report immediately

Use the support path supplied by the operator and include only the identifiers below; do not include passwords, API keys, payment data, platform access tokens, or raw confidential source material.

| Report now | Include | Do not do |
|---|---|---|
| You see another workspace's data or output | Screenshot with sensitive details redacted, workspace/content/run ID, timestamp. | Do not continue browsing, download data, or test other accounts. |
| Your output appears published without your explicit action | Content/run ID, platform, timestamp, visible URL if safe to share. | Do not attempt more publishing actions. |
| Spend/cap, approval, or reviewer state looks wrong | Content/run/gate ID, action attempted, result shown. | Do not retry repeatedly or alter records. |
| A generated output raises rights, safety, or platform-policy concern | Content/version ID and concise concern. | Do not publish the item. |
| A technical failure or stalled workflow occurs | Current stage, error text, timestamp, and whether you retried. | Do not share credentials or sensitive input in the ticket. |

## 5. Feedback expectations

After your first completed review decision, complete `BETA_FEEDBACK_FORM.md`. The beta asks about fit, friction, quality, time, trust, and willingness to pay only after you have used the workflow. Honest negative feedback is preferred to generic praise.

We will ask you for a short follow-up after your first workflow and again after 7–14 days if you have continued to use the product. Participation does not create a promise of permanent access, a feature roadmap commitment, or a publication outcome.

## 6. Access and data boundaries

Beta access is limited to your workspace. Do not invite additional users, upload another party's secrets, or use Lumora to make mass or repetitive output. The operator may pause access to protect security, spend, platform-policy compliance, or other testers. If you want your content withdrawn, request deletion through the operator; some financial, security, and audit records are retained for accountability as described in the data-governance policy.

## 7. Completion checklist

| Check | Tester confirms |
|---|---|
| I understand the beta covers one workflow, not automatic publishing. | Yes / no |
| I have named the person who will review outputs. | Yes / no |
| I will only submit material I may use and have the necessary rights to process. | Yes / no |
| I will not share credentials, access tokens, or other testers' data. | Yes / no |
| I will report safety, HRG, spend, tenant, or publication concerns immediately. | Yes / no |
| I will provide candid feedback after a real workflow attempt. | Yes / no |
